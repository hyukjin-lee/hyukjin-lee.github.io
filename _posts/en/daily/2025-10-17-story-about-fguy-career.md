I re-read [fguy’s 2017 post](https://blog.fguy.com/2017/02/being-a-programmer-part-2.html) about his career, and it is still fascinating. I had forgotten that he was heavily influenced by Kim Changjoon’s “Growing Together,” Kent Beck’s XP, and Bumjoon Kim’s Openmaru. The way he described Daum and Naver from that era also reads like a piece of Korean internet history.

It makes me wonder what kind of story I will be able to write fifteen years from now.
